# Email Sender Settings

The sender details are shared by all event emails: confirmations, cancellations and reminders. Find them in **FluentEvent** › **Settings** › **General**, in the **Email** section.

![The Email section of General settings with the sender fields](/images/emails/sender/1.-Email-Sender-Fields.webp)

## Fields

- **From name** (required): the name guests see, such as your business name.
- **From email address** (required): the address the emails are sent from.
- **Reply-to name** (optional): the name replies go to.
- **Reply-to email** (optional): when set, replies go to this address instead of the from address.
- **Email footer for event related emails**: text shown at the bottom of every event email. Add your business name, address, or a note on why the recipient got the email. It uses the editor with **Visual** and **Code** tabs.

::: tip
Use a from address on your own domain and set up an SMTP plugin so event emails are delivered reliably.
:::

Click **Save Settings** to keep your changes.
