# RSVP to an Event

How you join an event depends on its [entry type](/event-entry-modes-rsvp-and-open).

## RSVPing to an RSVP Event as a Member

On an RSVP event, logged-in members use the button in the event's details box.

1. Open the event from the feed or the **Events** page.
2. Click **Interested?**.
3. Choose **Going** or **Not going**.

![An RSVP event page with the Interested? button open to Going and Not going](/images/community/rsvp/3.-Going-Or-Not-Going.webp)

The button then shows your choice. To change your answer, open it again and pick the other option. If you leave an event you were going to, you are asked to confirm, because you lose your spot. You can register again later.

Visitors who are not logged in see **Log in to RSVP** instead.

![An RSVP event page for a visitor who is not logged in, showing Log in to RSVP](/images/community/rsvp/1.-Log-In-To-RSVP.webp)

## Registering for an Open Event

On an Open event, the button reads **Register**. Click it, enter your **Name** and **Email** and click **Register**. Logged-in members have their email filled in.

![The Register form on an Open event with Name and Email fields](/images/community/rsvp/2.-Open-Event-Register-Form.webp)

## What Happens After You Register

- On an RSVP event, a success message appears and the button shows your choice.
- On an Open event, a window titled "You're registered" opens with the event details and **Add to calendar** links. See [Add an event to your calendar](/add-an-event-to-your-calendar).
- You receive a confirmation email if the site has it turned on. See [Confirmation and cancellation emails](/confirmation-and-cancellation-emails).
- If the event is online, the **Join online** button now shows the meeting link. See [Meeting link visibility](/meeting-link-visibility).

## Events That Have Already Ended

A finished event shows an **Ended** badge and no RSVP button. Events with no registration (**None**) have no button.
