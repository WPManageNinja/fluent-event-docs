# Delete a Calendar

You can delete a calendar from its detail page. Deleting is permanent.

::: warning
Deleting a calendar also permanently deletes all of its events and their guests. This cannot be undone.
:::

## Deleting a Calendar from the Danger Zone

1. Go to **FluentEvent** › **Calendars** and open the calendar.
2. Click the **Settings** tab.
3. In the **Danger Zone**, type the calendar name in the box to confirm.
4. Click **Delete Calendar**.

![The Danger Zone on a calendar's Settings tab with the confirmation field and Delete Calendar button](/images/calendars/delete/1.-Calendar-Danger-Zone.webp)

The **Delete Calendar** button stays disabled until the name you type matches the calendar's name exactly.

If you only want to hide a calendar, set its status to **Draft** or **Private** instead. See [Create and edit a calendar](/create-and-edit-a-calendar).
