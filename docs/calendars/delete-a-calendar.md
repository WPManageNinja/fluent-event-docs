# Delete a Calendar

You can delete a calendar from its detail page. Deleting is permanent, so check what the calendar holds before you start.

::: warning
Deleting a calendar also permanently deletes all of its events and their guests. This cannot be undone.
:::

## Deleting a Calendar from the Danger Zone

1. Go to **FluentEvent** › **Calendars** and open the calendar.
2. Click the **Settings** tab.
3. In the **Danger Zone**, find **Delete this calendar**.
4. Type the calendar name in the box to confirm.
5. Click **Delete Calendar**.
6. Click **Delete** in the confirmation box.

The **Delete Calendar** button stays disabled until the name you type matches the calendar's name exactly. After the delete, FluentEvent returns you to the Calendars list.

![The Danger Zone on a calendar's Settings tab with the confirmation field and Delete Calendar button](/images/calendars/delete/1.-Calendar-Danger-Zone.webp)

A second box asks you to confirm. Click **Cancel** if you change your mind. The calendar is only deleted when you click **Delete**.

![The Delete Calendar confirmation box warning that all events and guests will be deleted](/images/calendars/delete/2.-Delete-Calendar-Confirmation.webp)

## Understanding What Happens to Events and Posts

Each event in the calendar is deleted with its guests. If an event was posted from the community portal, its feed post stays in the space as a plain post without the event card.

Deleting a space calendar does not delete the space itself. The next time an event is posted in that space from the portal, FluentEvent creates a new calendar for it.

## Hiding a Calendar Instead of Deleting It

If you only want to hide a calendar, set its status to **Draft** or **Private** instead. Click the status tag on the calendar's row, or edit the calendar. See [Create and edit a calendar](/create-and-edit-a-calendar).
