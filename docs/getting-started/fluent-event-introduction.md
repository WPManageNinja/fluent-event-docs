# FluentEvent Introduction

**FluentEvent** adds events to your [FluentCommunity](https://wordpress.org/plugins/fluent-community/) site. You post an event in a discussion space from the community portal, members see it in the feed and on the Events page, and they RSVP with one click. FluentEvent keeps track of the guests and emails them confirmations and reminders.

The community side of FluentEvent starts switched off. You turn it on once in FluentCommunity. See [Enable FluentEvent in FluentCommunity](/enable-fluent-event-in-fluentcommunity).

![The FluentEvent admin dashboard showing event and guest totals, a guest trends chart and upcoming events](/images/getting-started/introduction/1.-Admin-Dashboard.webp)

## What You Can Do with FluentEvent

- **Post events in your community.** Create an event from the FluentCommunity portal to publish it in a space's feed, in person or online, with a cover image, date, time and timezone. Events you create from the WordPress admin belong to a calendar and do not appear in the feed or on the portal Events page.
- **Collect RSVPs.** Choose how guests join: members RSVP, anyone registers with a name and email, or nobody registers.
- **Manage guests.** Add, edit, remove and check in guests, and filter them by status.
- **Organize with calendars.** A calendar holds events. FluentEvent creates a calendar for a discussion space when you post the first event in it, and you can add your own calendars for events outside your spaces.
- **Keep guests informed.** FluentEvent sends registration confirmations, cancellation notices and reminders before the event starts.
- **See the numbers.** The dashboard shows event and guest totals and a guest trends chart.

## How Calendars, Events and Guests Fit Together

| In FluentEvent | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Calendar** | A container for events. A discussion space gets one when its first event is posted, and you can create your own. |
| **Event** | A date, a place and a registration type. Posted from the portal, it is also a post in a space. |
| **Guest** | A person registered for an event, with a status such as Going or Checked In. |
| **Emails** | Confirmation, cancellation and reminder emails sent to guests. |

![An event post in the FluentCommunity feed with the date, location, going count and RSVP button](/images/getting-started/introduction/2.-Event-In-Feed.webp)

## Who Manages Events and Who Attends

- **Site administrators** create and manage events, calendars, guests and settings.
- **Members** see events, RSVP and receive emails.
- **Visitors** can register for an Open event with a name and email. Public and unlisted events in a public calendar get their own public page, and you can show all events on a page of your site from **Settings** › **General** › **Public Pages**.

## Features Not Available in Version 1.0.0

FluentEvent needs FluentCommunity to be installed and active, and WordPress 6.7 or newer. Events can be posted only in discussion spaces, not in course spaces. Paid tickets, recurring events and events created by members are not available yet.
