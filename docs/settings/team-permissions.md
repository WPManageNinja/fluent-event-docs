# Who Can Manage Events

In version 1.0.0, the **FluentEvent** admin is for site administrators only. Members and visitors can take part in events but cannot manage them.

## What Administrators Can Do

Users with the WordPress administrator role can:

- Create, edit and delete events and calendars.
- Add, edit, check in and remove guests.
- Change settings and email notifications.
- Post events from the FluentCommunity **Create new event** page.

## What Members Can Do

Logged-in FluentCommunity members can:

- Browse events in the feed, on the Events page and in a space.
- RSVP **Going** or **Not going** to RSVP events.
- Register for Open events.

Members cannot create events.

## What Visitors Can Do

People who are not logged in can open the public page of a **Public** or **Unlisted** event and register with a name and email, on **RSVP** and **Open** events. They can also browse the public events list. See [Public events pages](/public-events-pages).
