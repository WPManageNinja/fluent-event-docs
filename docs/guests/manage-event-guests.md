# Manage Event Guests

Every event has a **Guests** tab with everyone who registered. Use it to see who is coming, find a guest, change a status or add someone yourself.

## Opening the Guests Tab

1. Go to **FluentEvent** › **Events**.
2. Click **Manage Event** on the event you want.
3. Choose the **Guests** tab.

The tab shows a search box, an **Add Guest** button, the status tabs and the guest list.

![The Guests tab of an event with the guest list and status tabs](/images/guests/manage/1.-Guests-Tab.webp)

## Reading the Guest List

Each row shows one guest:

- **#**: The row number.
- **Name** and **Email**: The guest's details. Guests with a profile picture show it next to the name. Others show their initial.
- **Source**: Where the registration came from: **Manual** (added by an admin), **FluentCommunity** (the guest registered in the community) or **WordPress Pages** (the guest registered on a public page of your site).
- **Status**: **Going**, **Not Going**, **Checked In** or **Cancelled**. A checked-in guest also shows the date and time of check-in.
- **More**: A menu with **View**, **Edit** and **Delete**.

The list shows 20 guests at a time. When an event has more, page links appear below the list. A new event with no registrations shows **No guests yet.**

## Searching and Filtering Guests

Type a name or email in **Search guests** and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to find a guest. Use the tabs above the list to filter by status. Each tab shows a count, for example **Going (9)**. The tabs are **All**, **Going**, **Not Going**, **Checked In** and **Cancelled**.

If nothing matches, the list shows **No guests match your search or filter.** Choose **All** and clear the search box to see everyone again.

![The guest list with the status filter tabs and the count on each tab](/images/guests/manage/2.-Status-Filters.webp)

## Updating a Guest's Status from the List

1. Click the guest's status in the **Status** column.
2. Choose the new status from the menu: **Going**, **Not Going**, **Checked In** or **Cancelled**.

The change is saved right away, and the tab counts update. The guest stays in the current tab until you reload the list. A status change from this menu does not send an email. To email the guest when you change a status, use **Edit** instead. See [Add, edit and remove a guest](/add-edit-and-remove-a-guest).

![The status menu open on a guest row with Going, Not Going, Checked In and Cancelled](/images/guests/manage/3.-Change-Status-Menu.webp)

## Adding, Editing and Removing Guests

Click **Add Guest** to add someone yourself, or use **More** on a row to view, edit or delete a guest. For what each status means and how to check guests in, see [Guest statuses and check-in](/guest-statuses-and-check-in).
