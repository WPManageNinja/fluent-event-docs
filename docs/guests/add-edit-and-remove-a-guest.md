# Add, Edit and Remove a Guest

Guests usually register themselves, but you can also manage them from the event's **Guests** tab.

## Add a guest

1. Open the event and go to the **Guests** tab.
2. Click **Add Guest**.

![The Guests tab with an arrow pointing to the Add Guest button](/images/guests/add-edit-remove/1.-Add-Guest-Button.webp)

3. Choose how to identify the guest and fill in the form.

![The Add Guest dialog with Enter manually and WordPress user options](/images/guests/add-edit-remove/2.-Add-Guest-Dialog.webp)

- **Enter manually**: type the guest's **Name** and **Email**. Both are required.
- **WordPress user**: search by name or email and pick an existing user.
- **Status**: choose the guest's starting status. The default is **Going**.
- **Send confirmation email**: tick it to email the guest. It appears when the confirmation email is turned on in [Email notifications](/email-notifications-overview).

4. Click **Add Guest**.

## Edit a guest

1. In the guest list, click **More** on the guest's row and choose **Edit**.
2. Change the details or status and click **Update Guest**.

When you change a status, the dialog offers to send the matching email, for example **Send cancellation email**, if that email is turned on.

## View a guest

Choose **View** in the **More** menu to see the guest's details.

## Remove a guest

1. Click **More** on the guest's row and choose **Delete**.
2. Confirm in the dialog.

If you want to keep a record of the guest, set their status to **Cancelled** instead of deleting. See [Guest statuses and check-in](/guest-statuses-and-check-in).
