# Guest Statuses and Check-In

Each guest has one of four statuses. The status decides who is counted as coming and which emails are sent.

![The Guests tab with status tabs showing how many guests have each status](/images/guests/statuses/1.-Guest-Status-Tabs.webp)

## The four statuses

- **Going**: registered and expected. This is the default for new guests.
- **Not Going**: the guest said they cannot come.
- **Checked In**: the guest arrived and you checked them in. The check-in time is saved and shown on the guest row.
- **Cancelled**: the registration was cancelled, by the guest or by you.

Guests who are **Going** or **Checked In** hold a seat. Both count in the Going numbers on your [dashboard](/admin-dashboard-and-guest-stats) and in the going count members see.

## Check a guest in

1. Open the event and go to the **Guests** tab.
2. Click the guest's status.
3. Choose **Checked In**.

## Change a status

You can set any status from the same menu, or from the **Edit** dialog. Members and visitors change their own status by RSVPing. See [RSVP to an event](/rsvp-to-an-event).

## Statuses and emails

- A guest moving to **Going** from another status gets the confirmation email, if it is turned on.
- A guest moving from **Going** or **Checked In** to **Not Going** or **Cancelled** gets the cancellation email, if it is turned on.
- Reminders go to everyone except guests who are **Not Going** or **Cancelled**.

See [Email notifications](/email-notifications-overview) for the full picture.
